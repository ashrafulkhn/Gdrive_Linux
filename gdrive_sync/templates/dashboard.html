Step 1 of 2

Add your Google API client

Google requires every app that reads Drive to have its own OAuth client. You create it once, it stays in your Google account, and only this computer uses it.

  1. Open Google Cloud Console and create a project.
  2. Enable the Google Drive API.
  3. Under Google Auth Platform → Branding/Audience, choose External, add your e-mail as a test user, then press Publish app. Apps left in "Testing" lose their login every 7 days.
  4. Under Clients, create a client of type Desktop app and download its JSON.
